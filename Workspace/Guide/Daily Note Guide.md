# Daily notes

One per day, per person, at `Operators/<key>/Daily Notes/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Top three, not top five

The template asks for three priorities because three is the number you can
actually hold. A list of eight is a list of none, and it reads as productive
while committing you to nothing.

## What goes here

Live capture: what you are doing, what you noticed, what came up. It is a
working surface, not an archive.

Anything durable graduates out of the daily note the moment it is durable:

| It is | It goes to |
|---|---|
| a decision | a decision record |
| out of scope for now | the area's Parking Lot |
| a committed action | the area's `CLAUDE.md`, "What's pending" |
| a person fact | that person's note |
| a meeting | its own meeting note |

A daily note that still holds the only copy of something important a month later
is a filing failure, not a good daily note.

## End of day

Three lines: what landed, what carried, what is worth remembering. The third one
is the only place a week's worth of small realisations ever accumulates, so it
is worth the thirty seconds.

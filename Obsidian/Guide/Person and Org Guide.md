# People and organizations

One note per person in `People/`, one per organization in the same folder. Both
are entities, so they are wikilinked from wherever they come up rather than
described again.

## The two sections that earn their keep

**Who they are** should end with what this person is trying to prove, and to
whom. Job title tells you their function; what they are trying to prove predicts
what they will actually do.

**How to work with them** is the note you will reread before every call: how they
decide, what they respond to, what loses them.

## What not to write

The test is simple: if they read the note, would it be a normal thing for a
colleague to have written?

## Organizations are not customers

The template is `Org Note`, not `Customer Note`, and it asks for nothing but the
name. What the relationship is belongs under **How we relate**, in a sentence,
because the interesting part is what each side wants and no dropdown holds that.

A fixed list of relationship labels is the first place a generic template starts
describing somebody else's business.

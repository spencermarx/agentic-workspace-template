# Parking lots

One per area, at the area root, named `<Area> - Parking Lot.md`.

## Three registers, no overlap

The single thing to get right:

| The item is | It goes in |
|---|---|
| Deferred, or out of scope right now | the Parking Lot |
| A committed near-term action | that area's `CLAUDE.md`, "What's pending" |
| A load-bearing strategic unknown | that area's `CLAUDE.md`, "Open questions" |

Putting a committed action in the parking lot is how it gets forgotten: nobody
reads a parking lot looking for things they promised to do.

## Nothing is deleted

A triaged row moves to the Archive table marked `promoted`, `dropped`, or
`resolved`. A dropped item that keeps coming back is itself information, and you
only see the pattern if the record survives.

## Triage

Sweep it when an area's context changes, not on a schedule. The question for each
row is only: is this now a committed action, a real unknown, or still deferred?

# Research bundles

`Documents/Research/<topic>-<YYYY-MM-DD>/`, with a `00-summary.md` and numbered
receipts beside it.

## The summary is a pointer

State conclusions in `00-summary.md` and link the receipts that support them. Do
not put new material there: a finding that exists only in the summary has no
receipt, which means nobody can check it and nobody will trust it in six months.

## Bundles are immutable

New research lands as a **new dated bundle**. It never overwrites an existing
one, and supersession is noted in the folder's `README.md` index.

This is the rule people most want to break, because overwriting feels tidier.
The cost of breaking it is that you lose the ability to see what you believed
and when, which is the thing that makes old research useful rather than merely
old.

## Citing

Every numeric claim and named comparable gets an inline link to its source. A
number without a source is a rumor, and it will be quoted back at you as fact.

# The weekly cycle

Two notes, in `Operators/<key>/`: goals at the start, review at the end.

## Goals: name what you are not doing

The template's "Explicitly not this week" section is the one that makes the rest
true. A goals list without it is a wish list, because nothing has been traded
away.

## Review: facts before interpretation

"What actually happened" comes before "what I learned" on purpose. Writing the
interpretation first makes the facts fit it.

**What I got wrong** is the section that makes the rest worth writing. A review
with nothing in it is a review that was not honest, not a perfect week.

## Carrying forward

Carry what genuinely matters and drop the rest. An item carried four weeks
running is not a priority, it is a decision you have not made. Either commit to
it in "What's pending" or park it.
